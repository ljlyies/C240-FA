About StudySpark

Helping students who are struggling

Our Mission

To empower every student, regardless of background or ability, with personalized AI-driven tutoring that explains concepts clearly, builds confidence, and makes learning accessible 24/7.

Our Vision

A world where no student feels left behind. We envision a future where every learner has access to patient, encouraging tutoring that adapts to their pace and learning style. By combining AI with real learning materials, we're creating an educational tool that feels like having a tutor in your pocket.

Why We Built StudySpark

We noticed that many students struggle in school not because they're incapable, but because they don't have access to good tutoring or feel too embarrassed to ask for help. Traditional tutoring is expensive and often unavailable when needed most. We built StudySpark to change that.

Our AI tutor is designed to be patient, non-judgmental, and always available. It explains concepts in simple language, gives examples, answers follow-up questions, and recommends resources—all grounded in your actual learning materials through our RAG system.
